TITLE: West Ham United F.C.
West Ham United Football Club is an English football club. The club plays in the Premier League. It was started in 1895 and its home stadium is the London stadium East London. The club is also called "the Hammers" and "the Irons" because it was started in a London Ironworks. Their main rivals are Millwall and other rivals are Tottenham Hotspur, Chelsea and Arsenal West Ham currently are managed by David Moyes, their former care-taker manager for the 2017/18 season in Premier League.
